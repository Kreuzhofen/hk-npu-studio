\# Code of Conduct



\## Our Pledge



We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and inclusive environment for everyone.



\## Expected Behavior



\- Be respectful.

\- Be constructive.

\- Welcome new contributors.

\- Accept feedback professionally.

\- Focus on improving the project.



\## Unacceptable Behavior



\- Harassment

\- Personal attacks

\- Discrimination

\- Trolling

\- Spam



\## Enforcement



Project maintainers may remove comments or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.



\## Contact



If you experience unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer.
